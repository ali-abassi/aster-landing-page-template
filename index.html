Questions, answered

The useful things to know before you begin.

Still wondering whether Aster fits the way you work? Start here.

What does Aster actually do?+

It brings your goals, notes, drafts, decisions, and deadlines into one context. It then helps you identify patterns, compare options, and choose a useful next step.

How is it different from a general AI chat?+

A general chat answers the current prompt. Aster maintains a structured map of your work, sources, open decisions, and momentum so each conversation starts from the right context.

Does Aster make decisions for me?+

No. It makes assumptions and tradeoffs visible, suggests questions and evidence, and preserves your reasoning. You remain the author of every consequential choice.

Can I bring my existing notes and documents?+

Yes. The template imagines imports from common documents and note systems, with source links kept beside every generated observation.

Will my writing still sound like me?+

That is the point. Aster prioritizes questions, structure, and evidence over replacing your voice. Suggested text is always editable and traceable to your material.

Can I use it with a human coach or team?+

Yes. Share a focused snapshot of your map or invite a reviewer into one decision without exposing unrelated work.

What happens when my plan changes?+

Your roadmap changes with it. Completed work stays visible, new evidence is attached, and dependent steps are reordered rather than silently discarded.
